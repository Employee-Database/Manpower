1Life Employee Database

Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

This policy explains how the 1Life Employee Database web app handles information when an authorized user signs in and views the configured employee spreadsheet.

Information the app accesses

After you choose to sign in with Google, the app requests read-only access to Google Sheets. It reads the configured spreadsheet and displays its employee records. Depending on what the spreadsheet contains, records may include names, employee numbers, work status, job and department details, contact information, addresses, dates, and government-issued identifiers.

Signing in by itself does not grant access to the spreadsheet. Google also requires the signed-in account to have Viewer access to it. The app reads the configured spreadsheet; it does not write, update, or delete data in the source Google Sheet.

How information is used and stored

The app uses the spreadsheet data to display the employee directory, summaries, and reports for workforce operations. The page processes the data in your browser. In this version, the app has no separate application database or server that stores employee records. The app does not save the rows or Google access token to its own browser storage. They remain in the active page's memory while you use it and are cleared when the page is closed or reloaded. Editing or deleting a row in the page changes only that temporary view; it does not change the Google Sheet.

Services involved

Google handles sign-in and provides spreadsheet data through Google Identity Services and the Google Sheets API. The static web app is hosted by GitHub Pages, and the app loads typefaces from Google Fonts. These providers may receive technical information needed to deliver their services, such as browser and network request details. Their handling of information is described in their own policies: Google Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement.

Access, changes, and deletion

The spreadsheet owner controls the source records and who has Viewer access. To correct or remove a record, contact the spreadsheet owner or your 1Life administrator. You can stop the app from receiving spreadsheet data by closing the page, removing the account's Viewer access to the spreadsheet, or revoking the app's Google account access in your Google Account settings. The app does not keep a separate copy to delete.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated and its effective date revised.
